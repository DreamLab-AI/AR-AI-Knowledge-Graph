# ADR-2001 — Consolidate the documentation corpus onto a thin living ADR series

## Context
The docs corpus reached 241 files / ~489k words across VisionClaw and agentbox,
much of it stale, duplicated, or contradicting shipped code. Two independent
external reviews plus verified sense checks against the codebase confirmed that
readers could not tell which document described reality. Legacy ADRs are numbered
001–142 across both repos, so a fresh series must not collide with them. The old
corpus is too large to hand-edit into coherence in place.

## Decision
Freeze the legacy corpus as a read-only archive and start a thin, living ADR
series numbered from **2001** (no collision with legacy 001–142). Each new ADR is
at most one page, carries typed frontmatter (decision/implementation/activation
status, supersedes/superseded_by, verified_commit, owner, review_trigger, repo),
and must record how its implementation_status was verified against the code. The
index `README.md` is generated by `scripts/adr-index-gen.js` and never hand-edited;
CI fails on missing fields, bad status enums, or broken supersede reciprocity.
New decisions supersede legacy ADRs explicitly rather than editing them.

## Consequences
- One authoritative, machine-validated index; staleness becomes a CI failure.
- Every ADR is falsifiable — status claims are tied to a commit and a check.
- Migration is incremental: legacy docs stay readable but frozen; superseding is
  opt-in as topics are revisited, so no big-bang rewrite is required.
- Cost: contributors must keep frontmatter accurate and rerun the generator; the
  241-file legacy set is not retro-fitted to the new schema.

## Verification
implementation_status = partial: this ADR, the two `TEMPLATE.md` files, and
`scripts/adr-index-gen.js` exist and the generator validates this tree and emits
the index; the legacy 241-file archive has not yet been physically relocated.
Established at verified_commit 73540faa0 by running
`node scripts/adr-index-gen.js docs/adr` (exit 0) and confirming a
deliberately broken fixture exits 1.
